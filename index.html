From the first alert to the final forensic answer

I've spent 13+ years in security, most of it inside SOCs across Bahrain and the UAE.

Today I lead a detection engineering and threat hunting team delivering monitoring and advisory services to multiple enterprise customers, and I build incident response automation: isolating endpoints, disabling compromised users and revoking tokens without waiting on a human.

When detections fire, I work the incident through: scoping and containment, host and memory forensics, timeline reconstruction and root-cause findings that feed straight back into better detections. I'm trained and certified across incident response and DFIR, including GIAC GCFA, eCIR and eCDFP.

My work sits where SIEM architecture meets adversary behaviour: engineering data pipelines with Cribl to route, filter and shape telemetry so the right data lands in the SIEM at a sensible cost, writing detections mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, and proving they fire through purple team exercises.

What I do

Detection engineering

KQL, SPL and Sigma detections mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, tuned for signal and validated against real attack simulation.

Threat hunting

Hypothesis-led hunts across Sentinel and Defender XDR, turning findings into durable detections and coverage for customers.

Incident response & DFIR

Scoping, containment and eradication backed by disk, memory and timeline forensics, with root cause turned into new detections.

SOC automation

Automated response for endpoint isolation, account disablement and token revocation, cutting time from alert to containment.
